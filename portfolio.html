Hero of my own. I'm a BS Information Technology graduate (major in Programming) who works where AI systems meet the hardware they run on, from the machine itself to the networks and software running on it.

I also build the tools that make clinic work faster: data-driven web apps with PHP and MySQL, Python tools that turn raw reports into clear charts, and the hardware and networks underneath.
